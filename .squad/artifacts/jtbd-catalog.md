# Azure AI Foundry — Customer Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD) Catalog

> Compiled from six parallel research passes (customer service, enterprise knowledge/RAG, software engineering/DevOps, data & document processing, content/marketing, regulated industries + agentic automation). Every entry below carries a real citation; entries without an independently verifiable number are marked **Low** confidence rather than assigned an invented figure. Named organizations from the underlying case studies are described generically here (e.g. "a European bank") rather than by name; the outbound citation link still points to the original public source, which may itself name the organization. This catalog is the evidence base behind `.squad/skills/foundry-jtbd-routing/SKILL.md`, which the squad consults to route a stated customer goal to the right Foundry capability set.

**Coverage:** 83 candidate jobs across 6 domains. Confidence distribution is roughly: ~22 High, ~35 Medium/Medium-High, ~26 Low/Low-Medium. Treat Low-confidence entries as plausible-but-unproven demand signals, not committed roadmap bets.

**How to read an entry:** `Title — Cohort(s) — Signal — Citation — Foundry mapping — Confidence`
